# Ideas for later

**Reference — but deliberately not read by default.** Every lead should
know this file exists (`docs/sop.md`'s Locate table points here), but open
it only when the owner asks about something on it, or when you are
genuinely unsure what to build next and have nothing better to measure.
It is not part of the standing reading list; that is the point of it
existing separately from `docs/plan.md`.

This is a parking lot, not a backlog. Nothing here is scheduled, and
nothing here turns into a brief without the owner raising it first.

## What goes here

- An idea the owner floated and said "not now" to.
- A direction that was started and then stopped by the owner, with their
  words, so it is findable from here as well as from `docs/vision.md` or
  `docs/plan.md`.
- A loose end a review found that is real but not worth a step, with the
  reason it was left.

Not here: open questions for the owner (those go to
`docs/questions-inbox.md`), or decided work (that is a plan step).

## Entry format

```markdown
## <the idea, in plain terms> — <parked | stopped | tabled>, <date>

<what it is, in a few lines; the owner's words, quoted, if they parked it>

Revisit if: <the concrete condition under which it comes back, ideally
the owner raising it directly>.
```

Every entry needs a **Revisit if**. An entry with none is a backlog item
in disguise.
