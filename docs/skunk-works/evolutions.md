# Skunk Works — evolutions

**Reference.** Where Skunk Works ideas go after the owner has reacted to
them. The run reports (`docs/skunk-works/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<theme>.md`) are
snapshots and are never edited. This file is the living layer on top:
what the owner said about each idea, in their own words, and what the
idea turned into.

It is inspiration, not a backlog. Nothing here is approved, scheduled
or briefed. A lead or orchestrator looking for what the owner actually
got excited about should read this before the reports.

## How entries work

- **One entry per source idea**, headed with its name and where it came
  from (`report · #n`). Ideas the owner merges or spins off get their own
  entry under **Grown ideas** and link back to where they came from.
- **Owner's words go in verbatim**, in a blockquote with the date. Keep
  their wording. Tidy nothing.
- **Threads** are the distinct directions their reaction opens, one line
  each, written by Skunk Works. Put each in the thread's own words, not
  as a feature list.
- **Status** is one of:
  - *cold*: owner not interested as presented;
  - *warm*: liked, keep in mind;
  - *hot*: they're actively imagining it;
  - *pinned*: maybe, explore later;
  - *graduated*: moved into `docs/vision.md` (by Vision Partner, or with
    the owner's explicit permission). The vision section is then
    canonical, and this entry is only the trail.

  Status is Skunk Works' reading, so correct it when the owner says
  otherwise.
- **Later runs** add a `Research:` line when they find something new for
  an entry, rather than re-pitching it in a report.
- **Grounding claims are labelled.** "Probably buildable as pure data
  today" is a reading, not a verification — say whether it was checked
  in a running build.
